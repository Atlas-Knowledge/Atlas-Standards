# POL-001 — Governance Policy

> This document is **Normative**.

## Purpose

Governance defines how Atlas evolves.

---

## Policy Rules

Every normative specification SHALL:

- have an owner
- have reviewers
- maintain revision history
- follow the approval workflow

---

## Approval Workflow

Proposal

↓

Technical Review

↓

Working Group Approval

↓

Standards Council Approval

↓

Publication

---

## Compliance

Non-compliant specifications SHALL NOT become Stable.
