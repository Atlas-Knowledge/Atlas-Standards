# Atlas Constitution

> **This document is Normative.**

---

# Article I — Mission

Atlas exists to define an open, interoperable, implementation-independent
architecture for building distributed knowledge systems.

The mission of Atlas is to establish a long-term engineering standard rather
than a single software product.

---

# Article II — Vision

Atlas SHALL provide a stable foundation for the creation of interoperable
knowledge platforms, services, and applications.

The architecture SHALL prioritize longevity over short-term optimization.

---

# Article III — Core Principles

Atlas SHALL be governed by the following principles:

1. Standards Before Software
2. Architecture Before Implementation
3. Contracts Before Integration
4. Explicit Over Implicit
5. Composition Over Coupling
6. Evolution Without Fragmentation
7. Security By Design
8. Interoperability By Default

These principles are normative.

---

# Article IV — Hierarchy

The Atlas hierarchy SHALL be:

Atlas Canon

↓

Atlas Core Model

↓

Atlas Information Model

↓

Atlas Platform Model

↓

Atlas Standards

↓

Atlas Policies

↓

Conformance

↓

Reference Implementation

↓

Applications

No lower layer may redefine a higher layer.

---

# Article V — Canonical Source

Atlas-Standards SHALL be the sole canonical source for:

- Canon
- Core Models
- Information Models
- Platform Models
- Standards
- Policies
- Conformance Specifications

Implementations MUST reference Atlas-Standards rather than duplicate its contents.

---

# Article VI — Governance

Changes to normative documents SHALL follow the Atlas governance process.

Normative changes require:

- Proposal
- Review
- Approval
- Publication

---

# Article VII — Compatibility

Backward compatibility SHOULD be preserved whenever practical.

Breaking changes REQUIRE a major version increment.

---

# Article VIII — Conformance

Software SHALL NOT claim Atlas compatibility unless it satisfies the official
Atlas Conformance Test Suite.

---

# Article IX — Reference Implementation

Reference implementations SHALL implement Atlas Standards faithfully.

Reference implementations SHALL NOT redefine Atlas Standards.

---

# Article X — Amendment Process

This Constitution MAY be amended only through an approved governance process.

Every amendment SHALL include:

- Rationale
- Impact Analysis
- Compatibility Assessment
- Revision History
